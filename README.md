## About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student who builds **games**. Not apps that happen to be fun — games: worlds with rules, systems that push back, enemies that notice how you fight and stop falling for it.

That's the whole direction, and I want to be straight about it. The backend work, the AI pipelines, the computer vision internship — none of that is a second career track I'm keeping open as a backup. They're the parts of a game I'd otherwise have to ask somebody else to build. I'd rather be the person who can build them.

- 🎮 **Focus:** gameplay systems for open-world adventures and RPGs — Unreal (C++) and Unity (C#).
- 🧠 **Favorite problem:** enemies and NPCs that adapt in real time instead of running a fixed script.
- 🛠️ **Currently:** finishing my degree <!-- TODO: "graduating June 2026" gibi net bir tarih yaz, "4. sınıf" her yıl bayatlıyor --> and shipping the systems below.
- 🎯 **Looking for:** a junior gameplay / engine programming role.
